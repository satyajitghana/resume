## About — long version

I'm an engineering leader and deep-learning engineer who builds 3D computer-vision systems end to end — from LiDAR point-cloud reconstruction and custom neural networks to the cloud and frontend that ship them.

As Head of Engineering at Inkers Technology, I lead the team building our laser-scanning hardware and the construction-AI platform behind it. Along the way I've developed a modified SLAM algorithm that scans confined spaces and cut drift by 50%, a point-cloud cleaning pipeline that halved cloud thickness while preserving detail, and a thermal-camera integration that produces 3D thermal maps. Earlier in the same product I trained transformers for 3D mesh segmentation, shrank meshes from 1.2 GB to 120 MB so they render in the browser, and ran data pipelines processing 1–2 TB/day on AWS — including facial-recognition onboarding scaled across 2,000 parallel Lambdas.

I grew into this role from a Visual Processing Intern, shipping real-time edge AI along the way: custom GStreamer/DeepStream plugins running stereo 4K at 30 fps on Jetson, and YOLOv4-Tiny optimized 2× with TensorRT.

I also love teaching what I build — I designed and taught the EMLOV2 & EMLOV3 production-MLOps courses at The School of AI, covering everything from Docker and Kubernetes/EKS serving to LLM fine-tuning (PEFT/LoRA, vLLM, GGML).

🛠️ Core stack: Python, C++, PyTorch · SLAM, 3D Reconstruction, Point Clouds (PCL, Open3D, Blender) · Docker, Kubernetes, AWS · React, THREE.js · gRPC, FastAPI.

I'm happiest at the intersection of hard CV/ML problems and real products that reach users. Always up for a conversation about 3D vision, MLOps, or building engineering teams.
